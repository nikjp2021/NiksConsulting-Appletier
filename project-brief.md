# Project Brief: Nik's Consulting

## Brand Personality
- **Visionary**: Forward-thinking, embracing the latest in AI and agentic workflows.
- **Authoritative**: Expert, reliable, and deeply knowledgeable in digital transformation.
- **Sleek**: Premium, modern, and uncluttered. "AI-catchy and error-free."

## Core Colors
- **Primary**: Brand 500 (Deep modern teal/cyan blend)
- **Accent**: Vibrant Violet / Amber (for glowing highlights)

## Target Audience
- **Startups & SMEs**: Looking to scale rapidly using lean strategies and AI automation.
- **Founders & CTOs**: Seeking measurable ROI (e.g., 300% average ROI) through digital transformation.

## Core Conversion Goal
- **Get a Free Quote / Book Consultation**: Primary CTA driving users to engage for digital transformation and AI integration services.

## Animation & Style Preferences
- **Style**: High-tech but approachable. Dark mode by default, glassmorphism elements, neon glows (mesh gradients), and distinctive UI/UX.
- **Animation**: Smooth scroll reveals, staggering hero text, subtle floating elements. "Framer Motion" driven, no chaotic or overwhelming animations—everything must be GPU-composited and serve a UX purpose.
