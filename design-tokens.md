# Design Tokens

## Colors
- **Background (Dark Mode)**: `--surface-0: #09090b` (Deep near-black)
- **Surface**: `--surface-1: #18181b`, `--surface-2: #27272a`
- **Primary Brand**: `--brand-500: #0ea5e9` (Vibrant Cyan/Blue)
- **Primary Accent**: `--accent-cyan: #22d3ee`, `--accent-violet: #8b5cf6`
- **Text Primary**: `--ink-100: #f8fafc`
- **Text Muted**: `--ink-400: #94a3b8`
- **Border**: `--surface-4: #3f3f46`

## Typography
- **Heading Font**: `Outfit` (Modern, geometric sans-serif, authoritative yet approachable)
- **Body Font**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Highly legible, crisp)
- **Scale**:
  - H1: `4.5rem` (72px)
  - H2: `3rem` (48px)
  - H3: `2rem` (32px)
  - Body: `1rem` (16px)

## Spacing & Motion
- **Spacing Scale**: 4px baseline (`--space-1` to `--space-32`)
- **Border Radius**: 12px for cards (`--radius-md`), full for pills (`--radius-full`)
- **Motion**:
  - Spring config for Framer Motion: `stiffness: 300, damping: 30`
  - Glow pulse animations for background elements.
